# Research Brief: Mexico Property Data Market — Toluca/Metepec & CDMX

**Decision:** Enter or skip a data-as-a-service play scraping property listings, buyer/seller Q&A, and market intelligence for Toluca-Metepec (primary) and six CDMX delegations (secondary), to build alliances and monetize aggregated real estate data via a web app.

**Type:** market + competitive (named competitors for positioning)

**Geographic Focus:**
- Primary: Toluca - Metepec, Estado de México
- Secondary: CDMX delegations — Benito Juárez, Cuauhtémoc, Venustiano Carranza, Álvaro Obregón, Santa Fe, Cuajimalpa

**Time Window:** Competitor activities, regulations, funding, and traffic data from the last 12–18 months; earlier data as background only.

**Dimensions (priority):**
1. **Market size & growth** — reachable market for property data/analytics in these zones (not headline TAM)
2. **Customer segments & behavior** — who buys data: agencies, developers, investors, portals, proptechs, banks/insurers; how they decide, what they value
3. **Pain points & unmet needs** — what they complain about, work around, pay to avoid in current data sources
4. **Competitive landscape** — named competitors (portals: Inmuebles24, Lamudi, Vivanuncios, Propiedades.com; proptechs: Flat.mx, Homie, Yave, Casai, La Haus; data aggregators/APIs), including substitutes ("do nothing", manual scraping, INEGI/Sedatu open data)
5. **GTM & pricing dynamics** — channels, sales motion, accepted pricing models (subscription, per-lead, API call, revenue share), realistic CAC
6. **Regulatory & compliance** — data scraping legality in México (LFPDPPP, terms of service), INEGI/Sedatu open data licensing, portal ToS enforcement, anti-scraping measures

**Craft guidance:**
- 1–3★ reviews on app stores / Google Maps / Trustpilot for pain signals
- Public-company filings (BMV, SEC for US-listed proptechs) for industry sizing
- Competitor pricing pages directly, never roundups
- Funding history + job postings reveal competitor trajectory
- Complaint patterns persisting across years = stronger findings
- Try competitor products/trials where possible

**Freshness bars:** size/growth ≤ 18 mo · pricing & features ≤ 3 mo · behavior data ≤ 2 yr · GTM benchmarks ≤ 12 mo · regulatory ≤ 6 mo

**Two-source classes required for:** market size/growth figures; quantitative claims underpinning recommendations; competitor traction claims

**Deliverable:** A cited research.md + digests/ per dimension, ready for bmad-prd / bmad-spec consumption.
